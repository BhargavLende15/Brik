# Construction Material Demand & Procurement Forecasting

## Project Overview

An AI-powered construction procurement decision-support system that forecasts material demand, monitors inventory, predicts potential stockouts, and recommends procurement quantities.

## Business Problem

Construction companies must manage large quantities of construction materials across multiple projects. Poor demand estimation can result in excess inventory, material wastage, stockouts, emergency purchasing, and project delays.

## Objectives

- Analyze historical material consumption.
- Forecast future material demand.
- Monitor current and projected inventory.
- Predict potential stockouts.
- Calculate reorder points and safety stock.
- Recommend procurement quantities.
- Compare suppliers.
- Evaluate forecasting accuracy and potential operational benefits.

## Technology Stack

- Python, pandas and NumPy
- scikit-learn, statsmodels and XGBoost
- PostgreSQL and SQLAlchemy
- FastAPI
- Streamlit
- Plotly
